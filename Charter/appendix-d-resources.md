# Appendix D: Resources

*Further reading, organizations, and tools for abundance builders*

---

## Introduction

This appendix is a starting place for readers who want to go deeper into the subjects covered by Humanity's Charter. It includes foundational books, scientific papers, civic organizations, and practical tools.

---

## Section 1: Foundational Reading

**How this directory is chosen:** Entries are included because they explain a relevant idea, document a working institution, or offer a practical way to participate. Listing is not endorsement of every claim, political position, commercial product, or funding condition. Compare competing views, check conflicts of interest and accessibility, and verify a site's current activity before using it. Before using or contacting an organization, platform, handle, subreddit, or funding program, confirm its current activity, eligibility, and terms. Keep inactive entries in a dated archive rather than the current-action list.

## Section 12: Getting Involved

### How to Start

1. **Educate Yourself**
   - Read foundational books
   - Follow relevant organizations
   - Stay informed on developments

2. **Connect With Others**
   - Join local groups
   - Attend meetups and events
   - Build networks of like-minded people

3. **Take Action**
   - Volunteer with organizations
   - Support ethical businesses
   - Advocate for policy changes
   - Start local projects

4. **Use Your Skills**
   - Apply professional expertise
   - Contribute to open source
   - Mentor others
   - Share knowledge

5. **Stay Committed**
   - Build sustainable habits
   - Celebrate progress
   - Learn from setbacks
   - Pass the torch
